# eduGPT Product Blueprint

## Purpose

eduGPT is AiFrogi's education AI Business Bot for schools, colleges, universities, coaching centres, and training institutes. It uses the same sovereign, tenant-safe runtime as the rest of the portfolio while applying education-specific knowledge, authority, safety, and outcome rules.

## First verified outcome

`AdmissionEnquiryQualified`

The bot identifies the learner's programme, class or course interest, board or qualification context, preferred campus or learning mode, timing, and approved contact details. It then records a qualified enquiry or requests counselling. It does not claim that admission, a seat, a scholarship, a concession, or an academic result is guaranteed.

## Standard customer journey

1. A student or guardian asks about a programme, admission, fee, schedule, campus, facility, or policy.
2. eduGPT answers from institution-approved knowledge and asks only the minimum qualification questions needed.
3. The bot presents approved programme or batch guidance without inventing availability or eligibility.
4. The visitor requests counselling, a campus visit, a demo class, or an approved application link.
5. AiFrogi records the enquiry idempotently, routes it to admissions, and displays the verified outcome.
6. Sensitive, exceptional, safeguarding, grievance, or unsupported questions move to a human team member.

## Intelligence required during onboarding

- Institution identity, campuses, Google locations, contacts, logo, and approved photographs
- Courses, classes, programmes, subjects, learning modes, duration, and intake cycles
- Eligibility, admission stages, document checklists, and application links
- Approved fees, scholarships, payment plans, cancellation, and refund guidance
- Academic calendars, batch timings, facilities, faculty, and approved accreditation claims
- Prospectuses, brochures, policies, FAQs, and admissions scripts
- Student or guardian consent rules, data-retention boundaries, and safeguarding escalation
- Admissions team, counselling calendar, operating hours, ownership, and response SLA

## Connectors

eduGPT can begin as a governed knowledge and enquiry bot. Admissions CRM, student information system, counselling calendar, application form, LMS, payment, email, Website Bot, and WhatsApp connectors are enabled only when access is approved and verified.

## Safety and authority

- Minimise personal data, especially for children; involve a guardian where applicable.
- Do not collect identity documents, marksheets, passwords, OTPs, or payment details in ordinary chat.
- Do not make admission decisions or promise seats, scholarships, discounts, accreditation, or results.
- Use official approved sources for eligibility, recognition, fees, and policy claims.
- Escalate safeguarding, accessibility, grievance, special eligibility, and commercial exceptions.

## Product surfaces

eduGPT must remain available consistently in the public AI Bot portfolio, AI Bot navigation, trial registration, Super Admin onboarding, bot-profile configuration, intelligence blueprint, hero journey, installation guidance, sitemap, and production database category.
