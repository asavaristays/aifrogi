# One continuous customer journey

Approved direction: 45 seconds, same phone throughout, no actors, music only, short large captions. This supersedes the separate-screen montage approach.

1. 00–07: Logo resolves into the phone; room enquiry arrives.
2. 07–18: Chat flows into date and room selection.
3. 18–25: Confirm room; same Garden Suite stay confirmed.
4. 25–31: Same confirmation becomes a recorded booking outcome. No unrelated aggregate counters.
5. 31–39: Guest asks about early check-in; Team Inbox retains the same stay details.
6. 39–45: Phone eases back; logo, website, phone and email hold.
