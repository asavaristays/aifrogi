# AiFrogi Management Evidence Reporting Rule

Effective date: 2 September 2026  
Status: Mandatory operating rule

## Purpose

AiFrogi management reporting must communicate the actual product position without optimism, unsupported precision or incomplete evidence.

## Mandatory rules

1. Use evidence bands unless a reproducible scoring formula and sufficient sample are available.
2. Do not mix decimal ratings, percentage completion and maturity bands in one assessment.
3. Separate every material claim into one of four states: implemented, automatically verified, production-proven or externally assured.
4. A deployed screen or API proves implementation only; it does not prove the complete customer journey.
5. Synthetic tests must be identified as synthetic. They must not be presented as real-world accuracy.
6. Real-client accuracy requires a defined sample, reviewed labels, tenant/persona breakdown and calculation method.
7. A maturity band may increase only when the previously named evidence gap is closed.
8. Every operational claim about billing, support, onboarding, connectors or Super Admin must identify the corresponding verification evidence or remain marked pending.
9. Blockers, failed tests, incidents and limitations must remain visible until evidence confirms closure.
10. If evidence is ambiguous or contradictory, report the item as unresolved rather than choosing the more favourable interpretation.

## Standard conclusion format

Every management status report must state:

- what is implemented;
- what is automatically verified;
- what is production-proven;
- what remains unproven;
- the current launch gate;
- the evidence required for the next maturity movement.

## Governing principle

Product confidence follows evidence. Reporting must never run ahead of the product.
