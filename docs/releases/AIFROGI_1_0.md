# AiFrogi 1.0

Release date: 14 September 2026
Stage: Controlled Pilot Release

AiFrogi 1.0 establishes the first named product baseline for the governed AI Business Bot platform. It includes Core Intelligence, tenant-scoped intelligence, client-managed knowledge and flows, governed business actions, human handover, self-service configuration, Super Admin oversight, connectors, credits and renewal controls, tenant security, certification gates, and operational evidence.

The 1.0 name is a product milestone, not a claim of production scale or perfect answer accuracy. Pilot evidence and release controls remain visible and continue to govern promotion.
