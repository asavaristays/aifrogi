# Trust and intelligence boundary — 8 September 2026

This is a permanent working rule for future AiFrogi decisions.

The authoritative delivery order is `docs/AIFROGI_EXECUTION_ROADMAP.md`. Read it at the start of each work session and do not begin work outside its active phase without recording and approving a roadmap change first.

## Never confuse the two intelligence systems

### Sovereign / Core Intelligence

Shared across every current and future bot: tenant isolation, privacy, safety, intent routing, authority limits, evidence, handover, lead rules, failure controls and governance.

### Tenant Bot Intelligence

The individual client's customer-facing business understanding: verified structured facts, knowledge base, website, PDF and Excel inputs, approved trained answers, subject context, services, pricing, hours, location, customer-care number and natural useful responses.

Core Intelligence does not compensate for weak Tenant Bot Intelligence. A safe, governed or technically successful response is still a product failure when it misses an available business fact, gives an irrelevant answer, exposes internal scoring, asks sales questions too early or fails to help the client's customer.

## Required working discipline

### Hard sequencing rule

Do not develop, test or deploy Sovereign/Core Intelligence and Tenant Bot Intelligence in the same work batch. Every task must declare one layer before work begins and retain that boundary through acceptance and deployment. If work reveals a dependency or defect in the other layer, record it as a separate pending task and defer it until the active layer is completed. Do not combine their tests, success claims, deployment packages or progress reports.

1. Evaluate every change from the client's customer perspective first.
2. Confirm basic business answers before expanding architecture, tone work or test volume.
3. Never present automated PASS counts as proof of answer quality without human review.
4. Never expose lead score, grade, progress, governance, confidence or internal workflow language to visitors.
5. Keep lead capture behind genuine commercial or human-contact intent; answer ordinary questions first.
6. Fix a shared defect once in Core Intelligence, a subject defect once in its vertical layer and a business-fact defect only in that tenant's approved knowledge.
7. Do not run large question sets until a small representative sample proves that the evaluator measures usefulness correctly.
8. Report what is proven, what remains unproven and what customer consequence follows.
9. Preserve token and credit discipline: focused diagnosis, one coherent local batch, one build, one deployment and one meaningful verification.
10. When product fundamentals are weak, stop feature expansion and correct them before moving forward.
