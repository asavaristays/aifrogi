# AiFrogi Product Memory: Marketing Page Direction

Date: 3 July 2026

## Navigation

Public marketing header should use:

Home | Solutions | Onboarding | Integration | Resources | Pricing | Login | Start free trial

`/onboarding` is the authenticated product onboarding route. Public marketing navigation labels "Onboarding" but links to `/onboarding-process` to avoid breaking the logged-in customer flow.

## Page Style Rule

Future public pages should be clean, visual, and narration-led:

- No demo clutter.
- No unnecessary text boxes.
- Prefer crisp lines, real product visuals, and one clear idea per section.
- Use AiFrogi brand colors and the supplied logo.
- Use backend/product-inspired visuals where helpful, especially inbox and operations screens.
- Build pages one by one from the user's narration, image direction, and text assets.

## Solutions Page Direction

The Solutions page should lead with an inbox-style visual from the AiFrogi backend experience and explain business use cases in easy narration:

- Broadcast message
- AI chatbot
- E-commerce retargeting
- Reminders
- Payment collection
- Forms and surveys
- Review requests
