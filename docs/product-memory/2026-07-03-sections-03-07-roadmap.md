# AiFrogi Sections 03-07 Product Roadmap

Date: 2026-07-03
Source: product-owner discussion, `Aifrogi.mp4`, current production implementation

## Connected Product Flow

1. Section 03 - Activation: a business creates a trial workspace, verifies ownership, completes prerequisites, connects WhatsApp, receives its first approved template, sends a test, and becomes live.
2. Section 04 - Campaigns and Compliance: the live business selects an approved audience and template, tests the message, confirms consent and estimated cost, sends or schedules it, and measures outcomes.
3. Section 05 - Automation Engine: approved customer and campaign events enter observable workflows with conditions, quiet hours, retries, idempotency, pause controls, and human intervention.
4. Section 06 - Billing and Super Admin: subscriptions, usage, Meta billing evidence, incidents, customer health, support, and audit history become governable from the platform command center.
5. Section 07 - Commercial Launch: the complete journey is tested, documented, monitored, secured, explained through a branded product video, and released with measurable activation and support outcomes.

## Dependency Rule

Visual polish cannot substitute for a missing operating contract. A visual flow builder must not precede the durable automation executor. Paid acquisition must not precede registration, activation telemetry, billing enforcement, consent evidence, and restore-tested operations.

## Quality Gate

Each section follows Think - Rethink - Implement - Test - Achieve. A section is complete only when its behavior is deployed, production-safe, measurable, documented, and accepted by the product owner. Partial UI does not count as completion.

## Current State

- Sections 01 and 02 established the product shell, predictive dashboard, Inbox, human operations, and role separation.
- Governed Knowledge and personal team access are deployed foundations used by Sections 03-07.
- Section 03 is now in progress, beginning with secure self-service trial registration and owner activation.

Secrets, passwords, tokens, customer documents, contact exports, and runtime customer data must never enter product memory or Git.
