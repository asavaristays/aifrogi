# AiFrogi Interim Trust and Assurance Strategy

**Decision recorded:** 29 August 2026  
**Owner:** AiFrogi / webtechnosys  
**Status:** Approved direction; implementation and evidence collection in progress

## Position until formal certification

AiFrogi will operate and market itself as a security-first, privacy-aware Sovereign Business Bot platform. It will publish implemented controls, customer responsibilities, known gaps, and assurance progress without implying certification.

Approved public position:

> AiFrogi applies documented security controls, workspace isolation, protected credential handling, controlled support access, and auditable operations. Formal ISO/IEC 27001 certification and SOC 2 assurance are planned.

AiFrogi must not use “ISO compliant,” “SOC 2 compliant,” “ISO certified,” “government approved,” a certification logo, or an equivalent claim before the relevant independent body issues valid evidence.

## Trust Centre

The public Trust Centre must maintain:

- Security and workspace-boundary controls
- A dated assurance-evidence register
- Accurate certification status
- Sovereign Business Bot data and intelligence controls
- Key subprocessors and their purpose
- Shared customer responsibilities
- Privacy and data-deletion routes
- Security-concern contact
- Customer security-pack request
- Independent-assurance roadmap

Only evidence-backed controls may be marked Active, Published, Automated, or Verified. Planned work must remain visibly marked Planned.

## Customer security pack

The controlled customer pack should include the current security overview, architecture and data-flow summary, subprocessor register, access-control summary, backup and incident-response summary, retention and deletion position, available test evidence, and certification roadmap.

A Data Processing Agreement and binding incident-notification commitments require legal review before customer execution. Sensitive infrastructure, test, and vulnerability evidence is shared only under appropriate confidentiality controls.

## Immediate evidence programme

1. Maintain release-time health, security-boundary, and client-secret checks.
2. Record quarterly access reviews and credential rotation.
3. Record backup restoration and rollback exercises.
4. Commission an independent penetration test and track remediation.
5. Conduct and record an incident-response tabletop exercise.
6. Maintain a dated subprocessor and data-flow register.
7. Implement tested customer data export and deletion procedures.
8. Prepare the ISMS risk register, policies, internal audit, and management review required for future ISO/IEC 27001 work.

## Sovereign Business Bot trust principles

- The customer owns its approved business intelligence.
- Website and WhatsApp remain replaceable channels, not owners of intelligence.
- AI providers receive only the context needed for the approved task.
- Credentials are not exposed to the AI model.
- Consequential actions remain within configured authority and approval rules.
- Customer knowledge, conversations, actions, and outcomes are preserved as governed business records.
- Human access is scoped, time-bound, revocable, and auditable.

## Release rule

Every material public trust claim must have an evidence owner, review date, and supporting internal record. When evidence expires or a control regresses, the public status must be downgraded until it is restored.
